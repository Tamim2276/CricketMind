**Backup:** *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence* (Elsevier, 0952-1976), with
an impact factor of 9.0 (third-party). It asks for a real-world application validated on
public data (Cricsheet and StatsBomb are public). Its page lists desk-reject rules: the
abstract must state the AI contribution and the application separately; no undefined
acronyms in the title or abstract; single-column format.

**Suggested order:** IP&M, then ESWA, then KBS or EAAI. The supervisor decides.

## After choosing: read two recent papers from the first choice

Search the journal on ScienceDirect for "large language model" (2025–2026) and pick two
system papers. For each, note:

- How many baselines, and are they recent?
- Significance tests and confidence intervals: yes or no?
- A user or human study: how many participants?
- Ablation: yes or no?
- Code and data released: yes or no, and where?
- Length: pages, and how much is in supplementary material?

These notes go in `thesis/outline.md`, under "Target journal".
